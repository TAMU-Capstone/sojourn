# The Last Transmission

The probe's high-gain antenna has failed, leaving a constrained low-gain downlink. The radiation instrument remains operational, but its science data is being dropped in the competition for bandwidth.

Mission Control needs you to restore radiation reporting and improve the capacity of the low-gain connection without relying on the damaged high-gain antenna.

1. Recover radiation science by changing the telemetry priority data.
2. Reconfigure low-gain communications to carry the necessary science channels.

Inspect telemetry and memory before making changes. Your uplink writes are limited.
