# The Last Transmission

The probe's high-gain antenna has failed, leaving only the low-gain omni. The radiation instrument is still working, but its science never reaches the ground: there isn't room for it in the downlink.

Mission Control needs radiation reporting back, and then enough link capacity to carry the inertial measurements as well, without relying on the damaged high-gain antenna.

1. Get the radiation counter into the downlink by changing what the probe chooses to send.
2. Reconfigure the low-gain link to carry more science per frame.

Inspect telemetry and memory before making changes. Your uplink writes are limited.
